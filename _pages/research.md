My research lies in the area of **geometry and topology of manifolds**, in particular:

- Topological complexity and sectional category, including sequential and parametrized variants
- Geodesic complexity of Riemannian manifolds
- Topological methods in the calculus of variations, in particular the existence of closed geodesics
- Morse theory, Floer-type invariants and Hochschild homology of chain-level structures
- Applications of algebraic topology to robot motion planning

Much of my work connects classical tools from algebraic and differential topology — Lusternik–Schnirelmann theory, sectional categories, Morse theory — to concrete geometric problems such as the existence of closed geodesics or the complexity of motion planning algorithms on manifolds and other topological spaces.
